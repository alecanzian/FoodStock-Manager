# Project

The project consist of an application that offers an organized and centralized view of the food and house products in the house.

## User

Family members or people living under the same roof that want to track and know missing products wihtout having to communicate with other members.

## Goals
- Visualize list of products and their quantity
- Barcode scanning to chnage the scanned item's quantity

## Non-goals
- Notification push (to do later on)
- Graphs to visualize food consumption (not needed)
- Integration with bills to add products to the databse (complex and not related ed to the topic of development methodologies)

## Uncertainty (biggest risk)

The biggest risk is represented by the appplication's adoption, as the app could result in low usability. The users could find other ways to reliably find what they need to buy.

## Reproducibility

By folowing the README, you will be able to have a functional application that can be used in your daily life.
